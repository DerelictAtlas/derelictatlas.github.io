Embed a derelict on your site

Show the loot of one No Man's Sky derelict freighter on your own page. The widget scans the system in your visitor's browser with the Derelict Atlas scanner and shows the numbers you choose, plus a button that opens the full atlas. Nothing is installed. Privacy

Developer notes

Parameters of embed.html: glyphs (12 hex, the first digit is the planet and is ignored) or address (16 hex); galaxy (0-based, default 0); fields (comma list of modules, containers, crates, footlockers, briefcases, tall_lockers, rooms, decks, reward, loot, nanites, galaxy, hex; default all of them); theme (dark by default, light, or auto to follow the visitor); accent (hex colour); compact=1; button (button text).

Raw data: raw=1 shows no widget. Keep the frame hidden and read the numbers from the message below; the result also has url (the system's atlas page) and raw (the scanner's values, including the ship preset, stairs and hangars). The scan still runs in your visitor's browser, so the data arrives a moment after the page loads. Please link back to url wherever you show it. Open the frame's address directly to see the JSON.

More parameters: layout is feature (the default), tiles, rows, inline or cards; show_glyphs=1 shows the portal glyphs; wallpaper is a background id: void, steel, amber, cobalt, aurora, rust, lunar, grid, dust or crimson.

The end reward stays hidden behind a "Click to reveal" button, and is only included if you add reward to fields.

The frame posts two messages to your page (event.origin is this site; check it before you trust the data):

{ type: "derelict-atlas:result", v: 1, glyphs, address, galaxy, url,
  data: { modules, containers, crates, footlockers, briefcases, tall_lockers,
          rooms, decks, loot, nanites, reward? }, raw?, error? }
{ type: "derelict-atlas:size", v: 1, height }

Attribution: please keep the "Open in Derelict Atlas" button visible (you may change its text). It is always shown and cannot be turned off.
