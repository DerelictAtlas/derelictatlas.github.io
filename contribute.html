When the atlas reaches a million

        
Until the atlas holds 1,000,000 systems, every scanned system is saved with all its numbers: rooms, containers, Salvaged Frigate Modules, end reward and loot.

        
After that, only systems that break into a top-100 list in any category are saved: most modules, most crates, most value, most nanites, best end reward and the other lists. Everything else is still scanned but not kept, so the atlas stays small and fast while the best derelicts keep coming in.

      

      

        
What is sent

        
Scanning on this page adds the systems you scan to the atlas, with every number: rooms, containers, Salvaged Frigate Modules, end reward and loot. Only the 3D and 2D map is not kept (a map takes about 24 times the space), unless the system is in a top-100 list. Any system can still be opened in the viewer, which draws its map on your device.

        
To keep the atlas honest, your device sends just the addresses (galaxy and glyphs); the atlas scans each one again itself before publishing it. Nothing about you is sent.
