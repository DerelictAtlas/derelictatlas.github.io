How the atlas works

Scan on demand. The Derelict Atlas is a growing catalogue of scanned systems. A system that is not in it yet can be scanned on demand.

Enter your system’s glyphs or hex address. If the system is already in the atlas, its derelict opens straight away. If not, request a scan: the scanner works out the layout, loot and rewards, and the result appears here when it is done.

Some entries have summary counts without a full map. Module counts, loot and end rewards are predictions.

Systems start with the most predicted Salvaged Frigate Modules first. Scroll to load 100 more systems at a time. Use Filters to show only systems with maps.

Use Finished Run in the viewer after a run. Completed systems stay accessible with a greyed appearance; Filters → Hide done systems hides them. Run history is saved in this browser, so other browsers or devices have their own history. Press Finished Run again to undo.

Totals count every lootable container: salvage crates, crew footlockers, briefcases and tall lockers. Hover a total for the breakdown. “?” marks an uncertain prediction, “—” means unknown, and “+” is a lower-bound total (some counts are not known yet).

Both 3D and all-floor 2D are available in Viewer. Drag or pinch to inspect, expand either view, and tap a ladder marker to follow its connection.
